Questions people ask

Do I pay before I see anything?+

No. We build a homepage preview from the details you send. You pay once you are happy with the direction.

What do I need to send?+

Your business name, what you do, the areas you serve, your hours, 6 to 15 photos of your work, and your logo if you have one. We write the words.

Will I come up on Google?+

Every site is built so Google can read it, with a page for each service and area on the Business package. Nobody honest can promise you the top spot.

Is there a monthly fee?+

Not for the website in year 1. From year 2, hosting and your domain cost R999 a year. Monthly plans are optional: Care R99, Leads R249 or Growth R449, and you can cancel any time.

What do you build the sites with?+

We build lightweight sites around the features your business actually needs, with no pile of third-party plugins. That keeps them fast and simple to maintain. Every site still gets security updates, backups and checks from us.

Do I get a business email address?+

Yes. An address like info@yourbusiness.co.za forwards to your Gmail for free. A full mailbox can be set up for R250, and you pay the email provider directly.
