# Legal pages: review status

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

The privacy notice (/privacy/), terms of service (/terms/) and PAIA manual (/paia-manual/) have NOT been reviewed by a lawyer.
They were written from the owner's confirmed facts and the Information Regulator's templates.

Review them with a lawyer once there is revenue. In particular check:
- the liability limit in the terms (consumer law may restrict it);
- the refund rules in the terms (the Consumer Protection Act may restrict them);
- the retention periods and the list of service providers when tools change.

Also: /privacy/ and /paia-manual/ say the postal and physical address is available on request. When the owner supplies an address, show it on those two pages only, nowhere else on the site.
