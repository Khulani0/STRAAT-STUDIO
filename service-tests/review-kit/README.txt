Review kit: how to use it

1. Send 01-whatsapp-review-request.txt once, after the customer has seen and accepted their finished work. Replace [Customer name] and [Google review link]. Send it only to people who have said yes to being contacted.
2. If there is no reply after about a week, you may send 02-whatsapp-follow-up.txt once. Do not send a third message.
3. Do not offer a reward or a discount for a review, and do not ask only for good reviews.
4. thank-you-card-a5.pdf: print it and hand it over with the finished work. The square marked "QR code goes here" is empty on purpose. When the Google Business Profile exists, create a QR code from the review link and paste it into the square.
